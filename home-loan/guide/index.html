HOME LOAN GUIDE · INDIA

Home Loan in India – Finance Your Dream Home

Buying a home is a major financial decision, and a home loan can make homeownership more manageable by allowing you to finance an eligible residential property through structured monthly repayments. The right home loan depends on factors such as your income, credit profile, existing financial commitments, required loan amount, interest rate and repayment tenure.

Whether you are purchasing your first home, buying a ready-to-move property, constructing a house, or planning an eligible home improvement project, choosing the right financing option is important. With CredBaba, you can start your home loan application online by submitting your basic details through the application form. Your information can then be reviewed to help explore suitable financing options.

1. Home Loan Eligibility & Documents Required

Home loan eligibility generally depends on your income, age, employment or business profile, credit history, existing EMIs, repayment capacity and other lender-specific criteria.

Factors That May Affect Home Loan Eligibility

  • Monthly or annual income
  • Employment or business stability
  • Credit history and credit score
  • Existing loans and financial obligations
  • Age and preferred loan tenure
  • Property value and type
  • Proposed down payment

Documents Commonly Required

Depending on the lender and applicant profile, you may be asked to provide:

  • PAN Card
  • Aadhaar Card or other KYC documents
  • Address proof
  • Recent photographs
  • Salary slips for salaried applicants
  • Bank statements
  • Form 16 or income-tax documents
  • Business and financial documents for self-employed applicants
  • Property-related documents

Document requirements can vary depending on the lender, applicant and property.

2. Home Loan Interest Rate & EMI

The home loan interest rate directly affects the total cost of your borrowing and the monthly EMI you pay.

Your home loan EMI is primarily influenced by:

  • Loan amount
  • Interest rate
  • Repayment tenure

A longer tenure may reduce your monthly EMI but can result in a higher total interest cost over the full repayment period. A shorter tenure may increase the EMI while potentially reducing the overall interest outgo.

Before applying for a home loan, consider both the monthly EMI and total repayment amount, rather than choosing a loan based only on the advertised interest rate. Depending on the lender and product, borrowers may encounter different interest-rate structures, including fixed or floating rates. Always review the applicable terms, charges and rate-reset conditions carefully.

3. Types of Home Loans & Financing Options

Your financing requirement may vary depending on your property plans. Depending on eligibility and lender policies, different home loan options may be available.

Home Purchase Loan

For eligible borrowers purchasing a residential house or apartment.

Home Construction Loan

For eligible customers planning to construct a residential property, subject to lender requirements.

Home Renovation Loan

For eligible repair, renovation or improvement requirements.

Home Extension Loan

For adding additional space or making eligible extensions to an existing home.

Home Loan Balance Transfer

An eligible existing borrower may be able to transfer their outstanding home loan to another lender, subject to applicable conditions.

Home Loan Top-Up

Eligible existing home-loan customers may be able to obtain additional financing, depending on lender policy and repayment history.

The availability, interest rate, loan amount and terms can vary according to the lender and applicant's financial profile.
